# Site maintenance

Preserve the Japanese branding and the current GitHub Pages URL. The user prefers that AI handles product curation, verified Moshimo affiliate-code retrieval, copy, layout, checks, and uploads; ask the user only for operations that truly require their own account action.

Use `catalog.json` and `build_site.py` as the editable source, then regenerate pages. Preserve retrieved ad snippets and image contents. Verify exact product, seller, and package before adopting a code. Do not manufacture tracking links, reviews, tasting experiences, stock, discounts, or affiliate approvals. Do not count category placements as unique products. Normal purchases on at least one of Rakuten, Amazon, or Yahoo! Shopping are eligible; exclude furusato-tax rewards and nonfood lookalikes.

After changes, verify internal links, unique ad IDs per page, ad-button destinations, image loading, mobile overflow, search, and budget filters. Do not claim publication until the deployed site shows the intended change. Follow the user's existing authorization for routine updates; do not add unnecessary approval gates.
